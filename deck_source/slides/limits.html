Limitations

Where it breaks — and what we do not claim

Detection, not early warning

Alerts fire as works happen. We never claim to see stress before it is visible.

10 m pixels

Patches under ~0.6 ha and young plantings are missed, Bahrain's small stands included.

Imagery, not site visits

Conversions confirmed on dated sub-metre captures, not yet on permits or field records.

Simulated losses

Canopy-cover loss, not leaf stress; every rate next to its chance rate.

Hyperspectral

EnMAP confirms our indices but was not a better classifier (F1 0.74 vs 0.72).

Carbon

Area × field density from four sites (2013–14): a stock with a range, never a sequestration rate.

Rule we kept: no detection rate without its chance rate; no claim of advance warning, field verification or species discrimination.
