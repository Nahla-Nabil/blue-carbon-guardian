Validation 2 — Real Events

Five real conversions, confirmed on sub-metre imagery

Stand 5, 2021→2026: a reclamation with finger canals. Our alert on from Oct 2022, mostly from the cells that later converted.

Stand 9, 2020→2026: a canal-style development takes the south-east. Stand average “stable”; 8 of 28 cells in severe decline.

Stand 12, 2022→2023→2026: a new canal and reclamation take the southern edge. Found only by the cells; alert from Dec 2022.

Stand 18, 2020→2023→2026: the same development reaches it in 2025. Found only by the cells.

Dated 0.3–0.5 m captures (WorldView-2/3, Legion-1) confirm all four shown — and a fifth, stand 6, where a lagoon replaced part of the stand. At every site our alert began between the last undisturbed capture and the first one showing works — detection of works as they happen, not advance warning. All are partial losses: carbon figures are upper bounds.

These are not simulations. Stands 5 and 9 were singled out by two independent methods (the time series and an epoch-difference indicator); stands 12 and 18 only by the 200 metre cells.
