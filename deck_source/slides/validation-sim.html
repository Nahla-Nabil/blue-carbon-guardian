Validation 1 — Simulation

Can we detect a loss? Tested against pure chance

We injected artificial canopy loss into the real 2020–2026 series of stands with no known change, holding false alarms to about one per stand per year. A detection counts only if a new alert starts after the loss begins. The same test with no loss gives the chance rate.

Simulated loss Alert Detected By chance
A tenth of a stand loses half its canopy within 60 days 73% 13%
A whole stand loses 10% of its canopy within 6 months 63% 36%
A whole stand loses 20% of its canopy within 6 months 83% 36%

One alert rule for the whole product: the stand average OR any 200 m cell. The cells catch partial losses — how all four real conversions happened — that a stand average dilutes.

We corrected our own earlier test: it counted alerts that were already running, and it had no chance baseline (we had reported 58% / 90%). About 160–240 trials per row, so ±7 points. Out of sample (65 unseen stands, thresholds fixed): 0.79 false-alarm episodes per stand-year vs 0.96 calibrated.
