The tide looks like tree loss — until you correct for it

Mangrove canopies flood on every tidal cycle. One satellite pass mixes canopy signal with the water and mud underneath. On our stands, a wetness index alone explains most of the scene-to-scene noise — correlation about −0.9.

Our model regresses each stand's index on season, trend and tidal state before it ever flags an anomaly.
