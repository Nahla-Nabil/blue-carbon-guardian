Technical Innovation 2

A stand average hides partial loss. 200 m cells do not.

Stand 9 reads “stable” on average — but 8 of its 28 cells are in severe decline: exactly the part that converted.

Three of our five real conversions (stands 12, 18 and 6) were found only by the cells.

One alert rule: stand average OR any cell, calibrated together to about one false alarm per stand-year (0.96 measured).

A partial loss is caught within 60 days in 73% of simulated cases with cells, 25% with the stand average alone — 13% by chance.
