Business Model

A monitoring service with honest unit economics

Customers

Environment agencies (EAD, MOCCAE, Saudi NCVC)

Planting programmes: ADNOC (10 M), Red Sea Global (50 M), carbon developers

Coastal developers & EIA consultants

Offer · price (hypothesis)

Monitor: USD 12k per site per year (≈ USD 5 / ha)

Evidence: + high-res check of every alert, audit pack: USD 25k

Development watch: USD 8k per project per year

Cost to serve ≈ USD 3.3k / site / yr

Compute < USD 5 (measured: full 2020–26 archive in under 75 min)

High-res images only when an alert fires: USD 500–750 (vs 5–8k for monthly coverage)

Analyst time (assumption): USD 2k

Competition. Global Mangrove Watch offers free monthly loss alerts (Africa first, expanding; Gulf coverage not confirmed). We are complementary: tide-aware, calibrated, stand + cell level, with carbon and reports — and can use GMW extent as input.

The honest market

Gulf + Red Sea mangroves ≈ 16 sites → USD 0.2–0.4 M / yr: a niche. The real potential: a national MRV contract for a 100-million-mangrove programme, a monitoring layer on gIQ, licensing.

Prices and analyst time are hypotheses for the pilot to test. Scenario and all 13 sources: docs/business_plan_draft.md.
