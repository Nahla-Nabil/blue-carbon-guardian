1. No site visits — how do you know the losses are real?
→ All five are confirmed on dated sub-metre captures (WorldView-2/3, Legion-1, 30–50 cm). At every site the alert began between the last intact and the first disturbed capture. Matching permits and site records is our first pilot task.

2. Is it early warning?
→ No, and we say so. It detects works while they happen. We never claim to see stress before it is visible.  (slide 21)

3. Thresholds −1.5 / −3.5 — overfitting?
→ Calibrated only on stands with no change, to ~1 false alarm per stand-year, then frozen and applied to 65 stands it never saw, incl. Saudi Tarut Bay: 0.79 vs 0.96.

4. Is 73 % luck?
→ Every rate has its chance rate: 73 % within 60 days vs 13 % by chance, same test with no loss. We withdrew our own first figure when a null test showed it was inflated.

5. Why not just NDVI?
→ We tested it: same rule, same 25 stands — NDVI alone 1.61 false alarms per stand-year, ours 0.96, 40 % fewer; the tide model is most of the gain. All four setups catch the five conversions; the difference is false-alarm cost.  (slide 20)

6. Why does the tide matter?
→ At high tide flooded mangrove looks like dying mangrove. Index noise follows wetness, r ≈ −0.9. Modelling the tide cuts noise 29–35 %.

7. How accurate is your mangrove map?
→ Extent is not our contribution: ~0.6 F1 against reference points, and we say so. We start from ESA WorldCover outlines and focus on change and condition.

8. Did you cherry-pick the five events?
→ They came from the monitor's own flags; we checked flagged stands on dated imagery, including flags that were false positives, which we report. False alarms are measured separately.

9. What did hyperspectral add?
→ An independent check (EnMAP vs Sentinel-2, r = 0.84–0.99) and a material fingerprint: stand 5 turns from vegetation to a mineral spectrum — reclamation, not dieback. As a classifier it did not beat multispectral (F1 0.74 vs 0.72).

10. Why not Satellite 813?
→ Not available in the PoC (onboarding guide). Every index we use sits inside 813's 400–1700 nm range at 20 m; Sentinel-2 says when, 813 says what — tasked on flagged cells. Planned, not tested.  (slide 8)

11. Clouds?
→ Sentinel-2 cloud mask, only dates with at least half the stand clear; still 778 usable dates in six years.

12. Bahrain?
→ About 5 ha mapped, largest patch under 4 ha — too small for 10 m pixels. A case for very-high-resolution data in incubation.

13. Who pays?
→ Agencies (which stands to inspect), planting and carbon developers (is the stock still there), coastal developers and regulators (did works stay outside the mangrove line). Pilot price ~12k USD per site per year; cost to serve ~3.3k.

14. Different from Global Mangrove Watch?
→ Complementary. GMW gives free monthly loss alerts worldwide; we add the tide model, 200 m cells, a measured false-alarm rate and carbon at stake — tested in the Gulf.

15. Are the prices validated?
→ No — hypotheses for a pilot, labelled as such. Measured: compute under 5 USD per site per year.

16. Is carbon a sequestration rate / credits?
→ A stock, not a rate: area × field density 108 t C/ha with its range. It supports verification that a stock is still there, not credit issuance alone.

17. How does it scale?
→ Free data read in place, no downloads; a 2020–26 backfill per block under 75 min. Gulf ≈ 165 km² of mangrove — compute is not the limit; partners and site records are.

18. You are one person — can you deliver?
→ I delivered the full PoC; everything is public and reproducible. For incubation: a mangrove ecologist mentor, an agency or restoration partner, a GIS analyst.

19. Where is the AI?
→ Statistical learning per stand and cell: a robust model of trend, season and tide, and an anomaly detector calibrated against chance. Explainable on purpose — an inspector must understand why an alert fired.

20. First three months of incubation?
→ One pilot with a partner: match alerts to permits and site records, add Satellite 813 to the spectral step, deploy on gIQ.

FIVE NUMBERS: 29–35 % less noise · 1.61 → 0.96 false alarms (NDVI vs ours) · 73 % vs 13 % · 0.79 on 65 unseen stands · 5 of 5 conversions confirmed
